15 Years of Experience

Julia approaches each appointment with careful preparation and an understanding of how makeup needs to perform throughout the day.

Skin type, wear time, climate and comfort guide her choice of products and techniques, whether the assignment is a private celebration or a production. Her training includes study with leading Moscow makeup artists and continued professional development with Russian and European specialists.

  • /01

    Minimum Booking

    The minimum booking is R$ 1,500. Listed prices are final for the stated scope. The date, address, agreed look, ready-by time and any additional services are confirmed before the booking is finalised.

  • /02

    Reserved Time

    Travel to the first address, equipment setup and packing do not reduce your paid service time. For shoots, preparation, waiting time and movement between locations are included in the reserved hours.

  • /03

    Bespoke Quotes

    Travel outside Rio de Janeiro, intricate braiding, wigs, hairpieces or extensions, additional visits, complete changes of look and extra specialists are quoted individually. Any additional charges are agreed in advance.

  • /04

    Privacy

    Names, photographs, videos and behind-the-scenes content are published only with prior consent. An NDA can be arranged for projects.

  • /05

    Separate Costs

    Lash extensions, purchased accessories, venue hire and filming equipment are not included. The artist’s lighting is used for makeup preparation. Production lighting is supplied by the project team.

These details help us define the scope of work, preparation time and final price, with no unexpected extras on set.
